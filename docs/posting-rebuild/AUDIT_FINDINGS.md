# What is breaking — confirmed findings (29 Sep 2026)

Each finding was re-checked by a second agent against the code or the live database. 8 were dropped as unproven.

## High (31)

- **The board places cards by the post's approval, but every action uses the edit's status, so the lane and what the card does disagree** (board) — Dragging one of these cards onto Ready to post is refused with the toast "Already in Ready to post" while it visibly sits in another column. Dragging it onto the Draft lane, or choosing "Move to Draft — Send back for changes", opens the send-back dialog and sends the EDIT back to revision_required, when on this board Draft means the post is not approved yet. The Move menu offers columns based on a position the person cannot see.
- **A card whose post is not approved can still be marked "Booked in" from the board, which skips post approval** (board) — A handed-over or shoot card drawn in Draft or With client (post not approved, or waiting on the client) shows a filled "Booked in" button. Pressing it moves the card to Booked in with the client's approval still outstanding or refused.
- **A handed-over card stays in Draft through its whole posting run** (board) — Once the scheduler submits a handed card for quality check, or it goes to the client, it still sits in the Draft column. "Waiting on you" asks for a quality check on a card the board shows in Draft, and "Send to client" appears on a Draft card.
- **"Sent to client" appears on posts that were never sent to the client** (board) — The Posted card claims the client had the post, but it was never emailed or linked to them and a super admin approved it. The date shown is the quality-check pass, not a send. A card whose edit went to the client also reads "Sent to client" for a post the client never saw.
- **The drawer header says "Ready to post — Signed off. Needs a posting time." for posts that are not approved** (board) — Opening a card from the With client, Quality check or Draft column shows a green "Ready to post, signed off" header that contradicts the column the person just clicked in.
- **"Delete draft" does not delete: it cancels the post, which then appears on the week and month grids as a greyed "Cancelled" tile** (schedule-views) — The toast says "Draft deleted", but the post leaves the Drafts list and lands on the calendar as a Cancelled tile. No tile or row has a bin for cancelled posts, and dragging one is refused ("This post was cancelled, so it cannot be moved"), so it stays there for good.
- **A post's own status is ignored: with no job yet, every post on an item shows the ITEM's post-approval state** (schedule-views) — A new draft made from an item's leftover files shows as green "Approved" and appears on the grid straight away, though nobody approved it. The reverse also happens: a post whose own row says pending or changes reads as Draft once the item's field is empty, and it drops off the grid. The post's stage is taken from the item, which is the mix-up of post approval with item approval that the owner described.
- **The Stories strip and Stories view are always empty: they filter on an item content_type 'story' that no item has** (schedule-views) — The strip above the week says "Stories · none this week" and the Stories view says "No stories planned this week" even when a story is booked.
- **Changing "Whose accounts" does not re-filter the calendar (stale memo)** (schedule-views) — After picking a person in the owner dropdown, the rail filters (ownerMedia is memoised correctly) but the week, month, list, preview and the Waiting/Drafts counts keep showing the previous owner's posts until some unrelated data change recomputes them.
- **A 'duplicate' job (the provider says the post is already live) is not treated as gone out** (schedule-views) — A post whose only job is 'duplicate' shows the item's approval state (e.g. green "Approved" or Draft) and can be dragged. Meanwhile its list row's outcome line says "went out". The tile says it has not posted when it has.
- **Caption box loses focus after the first keystroke** (post-window) — On any post that is not already dirty, one letter goes in and the cursor leaves the caption. The user has to click back into the box to keep typing, which is the reported 'loses focus after one keystroke'.
- **A cancelled or failed post is a dead end: no button, and the controls left open do nothing** (post-window) — A cancelled post cannot be re-booked or retried from its own window. The user can change the time, words or media, but nothing saves them. 'Take it off' closes the window and the cancelled tile stays on the calendar. The only way forward, starting a fresh post from the rail, is never mentioned.
- **No 'Post now' for a draft, pending or changes post, while the error text says to choose it** (post-window) — Someone who wants a draft out immediately cannot do it. The picker has no 'now', the nearest quarter-hour is refused as too soon, and the refusal points to a Post now button that does not exist for this post.
- **The approval box says 'Approved — booked in for <time>' even when the booking failed** (post-window) — The manager is told the post is booked. It actually sits at 'approved' with no job and never goes out, and nothing on screen says so.
- **Approval is one gate per ITEM, so once a post is approved, a second post on the same card can never be sent or approved** (server-state) — On a card posted in parts, or any card that has had one approved post, the next post is stuck as a draft. Send for approval says 'already approved', Schedule says 'send it for approval first', and an account manager has no way through. Only a scheduler whose card was approved on the board (the viaBoard path) gets past.
- **Cancelling one post wipes the approval of every sibling post and can kill a sibling's live booking** (server-state) — Cancelling post A silently takes the approval away from post B. B shows as Draft, and moving or re-wording B's booking loses the booking entirely with 'Send the post for approval first'.
- **A cancelled (or failed) post can never be re-booked: there is no way back** (server-state) — A person who cancels a post, or whose post failed, has to rebuild the whole post (files, caption, channels, per-channel options) from scratch. The card sits in Ready to post showing a dead, cancelled post.
- **The approval write is check-then-write, so two answers at once both land** (server-state) — A client pressing Approve on the portal while a manager presses Ask for a change can both succeed. The last write wins, both sets of emails go out, and bookApprovedPosts may already have booked the post the manager just rejected.
- **The portal can approve a post that was never put to the client** (server-state) — Anyone holding the client's portal link can approve or reject an internal-only post approval by posting its item id. The post is then booked in (bookApprovedPosts, :255) without the account manager ever deciding.
- **Nothing is frozen at send: the client approves whatever the post holds at the moment they press Approve** (server-state) — The client portal shows files or words that changed after the email was sent, while the card still says 'Emailed … <date>'. The client's yes then applies to content they were never sent.
- **New media on a pending post un-sends the post but leaves the item pending, so the client's yes books nothing** (server-state) — The Post approval card says 'waiting on the client', the client approves, and the post stays a Draft on Schedule with nothing booked and nobody told.
- **Deleting a card leaves a provider-held booking live when the card is still in Ready to post** (server-state) — Deleting a part-booked card removes it from every screen while the provider still publishes the booked post, and nothing in the app shows it any more. Live: 46 social_posts point at deleted items, including 'scheduled' posts with failed jobs (fc3ee75b, 4e742215) that no calendar will ever show (listPosts filters them out, :2043).
- **Nothing is frozen at send: the client sees files added after the card was sent to them** (portal) — The client approves or comments on files they were never sent, and the version the email invited them to review cannot be reconstructed afterwards.
- **Post approval page reads the social_post live, so what the client approves is not what was sent** (portal) — The caption, pictures or time in the email can differ from the page the client presses Approve on. Their yes is recorded against whatever the post is at that moment.
- **A client can approve a post that was only sent for internal (team) approval** (portal) — A post waiting on the account manager can be approved from the client's share link. The team board then moves it to Ready to post, skipping the internal approval.
- **?preview=1 is not gated, and it shows posts waiting on the team to the client** (portal) — Anyone with the client's link can see an internal-only post, with its caption, time and networks, before the team has passed it to the client.
- **Every Published card opens a page saying 'Approved — thank you. We'll book it in to go out'** (portal) — On a post that is already live, the client is told they approved it and that it will be booked in, which is untrue on both counts.
- **The approval link emailed to the client later shows their approval, though the team approved it** (portal) — Jordan opens the link he was emailed and reads 'Approved — thank you', but he never answered. The reset also left the client_sent stamp in place.
- **An unsent upload's picture becomes the client's portal hero backdrop** (portal) — Work the client has never been sent is shown full-bleed at the top of their portal.
- **A post on several networks gets one network's link on every network's schedule row** (live-data) — The client portal lists each network with its live link (portal-data.ts:465). A client or AM who clicks 'Instagram' or 'LinkedIn' lands on the TikTok or Instagram post instead. Every multi-network post in September is affected.
- **A network that never posted is marked Published** (live-data) — The portal and any report built on schedule_entries say this carousel went out on Instagram. It never did. Nobody will notice that Instagram is still owed.

## Medium (35)

- **"The client asked for a change" appears when a manager asked for the change** (board) — When an account manager reviews a scheduler's post internally and asks for a change, the card tells the scheduler the CLIENT asked for it.
- **On a post waiting on the client, the card says so but its main button is "Approve the post"** (board) — A manager sees "A post is waiting on the client" directly above a black "Approve the post" button. One press records the manager's own yes as the post approval (useCardActs.tsx:61-73; the route then books the post) before the client has answered.
- **A post the client asked to change never appears in "Waiting on you"** (board) — The post the client sent back sits in Draft. The panel above the board, which is meant to hold everything someone is held up by, leaves it out, so nobody is prompted to fix it and resend.
- **The success toast names a column the card does not land in** (board) — The toast says "now in Ready to post" and the card appears in Draft.
- **List view shows one column in "Column" and a different one in "Stage"** (board) — Each row gives two different answers to where the post is.
- **A card moved to Draft by postingColumn shows no reason for being there** (board) — A finished, approved edit whose post has not been sent for approval yet sits in Draft with no chip and no line explaining it. It looks like work that is still being made, and its only button may be "Booked in".
- **Overview counts and lenses that link into this board use the raw status, so the numbers don't match the board** (board) — For a general user the Overview says 12 ready to post and the Ready to post column shows 8. 'With clients' leaves out the 2 posts waiting on Jordan. "Waiting on an account" includes posts that are not ready.
- **Posts waiting on approval after they are booked stay in Booked in** (board) — A booked post that is edited and needs approval again, or is waiting on the client, stays under "Booked in", a column whose header says the channel has it.
- **When a job fails or is cancelled, social_posts.status stays 'scheduled', so the rail keeps treating those files as used** (schedule-views) — After a post fails, the tile tells the person to start a new one, but the rail has taken those files out as "used". The failed post cannot be dragged or re-booked from the rail, so there is no way forward. (The four live rows above belong to deleted items today, so they show the stuck status but not the rail symptom on screen.)
- **A partly posted job shows as "Did not go out" on the tile and in the list row's status** (schedule-views) — A post that is live on three of four networks shows a red-outline "Did not go out" tile. In the List the same row says "Did not go out" next to "✓ Instagram … went out", so the row contradicts itself.
- **Preview's "feed as it will look" includes cancelled, failed, non-Instagram and already-published posts, and posted ones appear twice** (schedule-views) — The planned feed shows cancelled and failed posts as if they will appear. With no channel picked it also shows TikTok- or LinkedIn-only posts. A published post shows once as a planned tile and again as the real post below it.
- **Posts whose item was deleted disappear from the calendar, including 13 marked 'scheduled' (mostly past, 11 of them published) and a failed one** (schedule-views) — Posting history and failures vanish from Schedule once the piece is removed. A failure from 24 Sep is shown nowhere on the calendar.
- **The Edit media launcher saves to the item's FIRST post, which may be a cancelled or finished one, so the edit misses the open post** (schedule-views) — After an image is edited from the week toolbar, the post that is still open keeps its old slides. The editor still reports it saved.
- **The rail's "Remove this piece" can only be reached on cards that can already be posted** (schedule-views) — A greyed card ("No media yet", "With the client now", deliver-only) cannot be removed from Schedule by a manager. It stays in the rail with no action.
- **'This post is waiting on you' is shown to the manager when the post is with the client** (post-window) — After a manager sends a post to the client, reopening it still says the post is waiting on the manager and offers Approve. That label hides who actually has it.
- **The Post approval ('reviewOnly') path in the window is unreachable, and would be refused if it ran** (post-window) — The window behaves the same whether it is thought of as opened from Schedule or from Post approval. The comments and code describe a Post approval mode that does not exist, and wiring it up as written would show a red refusal on every content post.
- **The media picker shows old window errors as 'Not saved yet' before any save** (post-window) — If an earlier press (Schedule, Send) failed, opening Change media immediately shows 'Not saved yet' with that unrelated refusal, before the user has saved anything.
- **'Sent to client' is shown for cards whose post was never sent, because client_sent is never cleared** (server-state) — Post approval and Posted cards say 'Sent to client' or 'Emailed … <date>' about a post the client never saw. A second round re-uses the first round's stamp (sentForStage only matches the stage), so 'emailed' shows before anyone has sent anything.
- **The stored social_posts.status is never advanced to published or failed, and several readers trust it** (server-state) — The client email can quote an already-published sibling's caption or time. The portal can refuse a client's approval with 'the time has passed' because of an old, already-published post. Card lines disagree with the calendar, which uses live_status.
- **Re-send jobs are not linked to their post, so the post's status and cancel ignore them** (server-state) — While a re-send is queued the tile shows the parent's outcome (Failed or Published), and cancelling the post leaves the queued re-send free to go out.
- **Saving a draft emails the managers that it is being published or scheduled** (server-state) — Account managers get 'Publishing now' or 'Scheduled for …' emails for drafts that nobody has approved or booked.
- **Post approval is tied to the edit approval, not independent of it** (server-state) — A post uploaded straight from Schedule (which is itself the post) cannot be sent for post approval until it has passed the editor/designer edit flow, and pressing Schedule quietly moves the edit's own approval state.
- **The booking email goes out before the booking exists** (server-state) — Managers are told a post is booked when the provider refused it.
- **schedulePost can leave a post 'scheduled' with no job** (server-state) — After a network error the post shows as booked but nothing is at the provider. updatePost says 'already booked — cancel it first' and Schedule says 'already booked'; only Cancel gets it out.
- **One published part marks every schedule row of the card published** (server-state) — On a card posted in parts, the later parts' times show as posted on the board and portal. moveScheduleRows and cancelPost then skip those rows (social-schedule.ts:1909, :1971), so moving or cancelling the later parts no longer updates their times.
- **The board-path clearance overwrites the gate without a claim** (server-state) — A client's or manager's 'changes' or 'pending' answer that lands at the same moment is overwritten to approved, and the post is booked anyway.
- **Full board payload, including in-production and unsent cards, is shipped to the browser** (portal) — Titles of drafts, and media and links of posts never sent, can be read from the page source. They also supply the item ids that the preview and approve_post holes above need.
- **The client portal shows a post as 'waiting on you' before anyone has emailed it** (portal) — The client finds an approval request on their page that nobody sent. The team board's 'Sent to client' (board-view-core.ts:267 counts posting_client_required) agrees with it, so both sides say it was sent.
- **Edit-stage notes are pinned to the post's slides by index** (portal) — A note about clip 3 of the edit shows up on picture 3 of the final post, which mixes the edit approval with the post approval that the owner says are independent.
- **Home card frames and the approval page can show different posts** (portal) — When an item has a cancelled post or a re-timed copy, the preview frames on the portal home can come from a different post (or a cancelled one) than the one the client approves. If the frames fail, the home card shows no caption.
- **Portal column disagrees with the team's posting board for approved posts** (portal) — The client sees 'Approved' for a post the team shows in Draft, or for one only the team approved. The same unsent upload is hidden while at Ready to post and appears as 'Going out…' the moment it is booked.
- **'Thanks — we have your note' and 'We're making your changes' show when the team asked for the change** (portal) — A client who never commented is thanked for their note.
- **social_posts.status stays 'scheduled' after the post is live** (live-data) — The raw row says a post is still booked when it has already gone out. Any code that reads it without the jobs (cancel, send-to-client, reports) sees a live booking that no longer exists.
- **Cards at With client read 'Passed' because they reached the client before the round stamp existed** (live-data) — Cards sitting in the With client column tell the team they were only passed internally. This is the reverse of the old 'Sent to client' lie.
- **An edit card being revised shows on Post approval in Draft** (live-data) — Post approval shows an editor's card that is back in revision after client changes. It sits in Draft labelled 'Sent to client 24 Sept', which mixes the edit's approval with the post's.
