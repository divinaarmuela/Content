Shoots and Editing Scenarios

Every situation the Shoots page and the Editor page are built to handle, what you press, what the app does by itself, who is told, and what shows on screen. Written to the Team's Playbook (July 2026) and the quality-review rule of 11 September 2026: account manager or designer or editor, then Joy, then the scheduler.

The shoot, stage by stage

DraftShared with teamConfirmedReminder sentShoot dayFootage in

Blue stages move by themselves on the calendar. The others move when a person presses the one button under "Where it is" on the shoot page. Nothing needs to be dragged; dragging on the board is a second way to do the same thing.

Whose page it is. The shoot page and every button on it belong to the account manager on the client, the person who created the shoot, a super admin and a general user. Editors, schedulers and crew never open it: the plan reaches them in the email and on the editor's card, and an old link sends them to their Editor page. Every stamp on the page says who did it and when ("Shared with the team by Ada, Mon 14 Sept 9:10 am"), and "Created by Ada" sits under the title and on the board card.
The one-week rule. The plan must be shared with the team at least 7 days before the shoot. A late plan turns the card red, tells the AM and Abby once a day, and blocks Go. Only a super admin can go ahead late, with a reason that stays on the card.

The editor, card by card

In progressFor reviewQuality checkWith clientFor handoffDone

These are the Editor page columns. An editor or designer sees only their own cards. An account manager sees every card for their clients; a super admin every card.

One shoot, one card. Finals only. A shoot makes one card for the editor, holding every final as a file. A card takes pictures and videos. Raw footage, project files and source files stay on the hard drive and Dropbox; the card carries a link to them. Nothing reaches a scheduler without Joy or a super admin passing it.

Who sees what

Shoots

  • Super admin: every shoot, every move.
  • Account manager: their clients' shoots, every move.
  • General: every shoot; can create, staff and move.
  • Editor or crew: the shoots they are named on, on the board only. They never open the shoot page — the plan is on the editor's card and in the email, and "I've read the plan" is pressed there. Nothing on the shoot page is theirs: not the footage folder, not Footage is in.
  • Scheduler: only if named as crew, the same way.
  • The creator of a shoot works its page whatever their role.
  • Client: the plan on their portal when it is shared with them; never the team's stages.

Editor and Post approval

  • Super admin: every card; may pass quality check.
  • Account manager: their clients' cards; checks and sends to Joy.
  • Quality reviewer (Joy): every card in Quality check for every client, plus their own role's cards.
  • Editor or designer: only cards they own, through every stage.
  • General: their own cards; every client's Ready to post queue.
  • Scheduler: cards handed to them from Ready to post on, plus their own uploads. Never a deliver-only card.
  • Client: their own cards on the portal as Being made, Being checked, Needs your review, Changes in progress, Approved, Delivered or Posted.

Set up once

  • Team page: switch on Quality reviewer for Joy and Ops contact for Abby. Until Joy is flagged, every quality check goes to the super admins and a banner says so.
  • Each client's page: Who schedules for this client (Cath, Raven), and This client posts their own content where that is true.
  • Editors and designers hold the editor role; schedulers the scheduler role. Joy keeps whatever role she has.

Every step above writes a line in the card's or shoot's "What happened" with who and when. The whole chain from New shoot plan to Posted is re-run on the live database against the test client before each release.
