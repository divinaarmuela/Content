The Shoots page, every route

One card is one shoot. The card moves along six columns, and this guide is every way it can move, what you press, what has to be true first, and who is told. Written from the app as it runs on 13 September 2026.

The normal road, step by step

1 · Draft: write the plan

Press New shoot plan, name it, pick the client and the date. The plan has nine parts: objective, deliverables, shot list, script or talking points, date with call time and location, talent, props and wardrobe, client availability, editor priorities and deadline. The card shows "3 of 9 filled" until all nine are in.

Who is on this shoot: add the editor and the crew here. Nobody is emailed yet. Paste the footage folder link (Dropbox or Drive) whenever you have it.

The plan is due 7 days before the shoot. Under 7 days and still in Draft, the card turns red with "Plan is late" and lists what is missing. Once, the account managers on the client and the Ops contact get ⚠️ Plan is late.

2 · Share the plan with the team

Press Share the plan with the team. Needs the nine parts filled. What happens:

  • The shoot's one editor card is created on the Editor page, in In Progress, in the editor's name if one is named. It carries the whole plan.
  • The editor gets Read the plan with a link to that card, where they press "I've read the plan".
  • Everyone else on the shoot gets Read the plan with an acknowledge link. One press marks them as read.
  • The card reads "read 1 of 3" until everyone has.

If the share lands under 7 days before the shoot, the managers and Ops get ⚠️ Plan shared late and Go will need a super admin.

3 · Confirmed: Go books the shoot

Press Confirm — it is go. Refused, with the reason under the button, until all of these are true:

  • the nine parts are filled;
  • "Aligned with the strategist or creative director" is ticked;
  • "Client availability and location confirmed" is ticked;
  • everyone on the shoot has acknowledged the plan;
  • the plan was shared 7 or more days before the shoot, or a super admin presses Go anyway with a reason (the managers and Ops are emailed the reason).

Go books the shoot and stamps who pressed it and when. The editor gets You're on [shoot] — your card is ready, shoot on [date]. The card on the Editor page now reads "Shoot on [date], footage after that".

4 · Reminder sent

Press Reminder sent to everyone. Everyone on the shoot, plus the creator, gets Shoot reminder: [shoot] — [date]. Only allowed after Go.

5 · Shoot day (the calendar's column)

On the shoot date the card moves here by itself. Nobody can drag a card in or out of this column. To move it, change the shoot date.

6 · Footage in: the handover to the editor

Two ways, and they never both happen:

  1. You press "Footage is in" on the shoot page, the day of the shoot or after. Refused until: the shoot date has passed, an editor is named, priorities and deadline are written, and the footage folder link is pasted. The editor gets Footage is in: [shoot] — your card is ready, due [deadline] with the folder link and the priorities. The folder appears on their card under "Work from".
  2. You do nothing. The morning after the shoot the app hands it over by itself. The editor gets Footage should be in: [shoot] — start the edit. If no folder link was pasted, the email tells them to ask you, and pasting it later still reaches their card.

From here the work is on the Editor page and the shoot card sits in Footage in. It stays there. There is no "close" button; the shoot is done when its edits are done.

Routes off the normal road

The shoot happened and you have not decided who edits it

"Let's say we have a shoot but I am not sure yet who is editing, but the shoot is finished. What happens?"

  1. The card sits in Shoot day. "Footage is in" is greyed: Name the editor on the shoot first — the footage is handed to them.
  2. The morning after the shoot, the app cannot hand over to nobody. Instead, once, it emails the account managers and super admins on the client: Name the editor: [shoot] was shot and nobody has the footage, with a link to the shoot.
  3. Nothing else moves. No editor card is made in anyone's name. If the plan was shared, the shoot's card already exists on the Editor page with no owner. Any editor can open it, but it is nobody's.
  4. When you decide, open the shoot, pick the editor under Who is on this shoot. The moment you save:
    • the card becomes theirs, with the due date and priorities from the plan;
    • they get You're on [shoot] — your card is ready.
  5. Then press Footage is in (paste the folder link first). They get Footage is in and start. The automatic morning-after handover does not run a second time for this shoot, so this press is yours to make.

If the shoot was never shared with the team (still Draft when it was shot), the same applies, and there is no editor card until you either share the plan or name an editor.

The editor is named, but priorities or the deadline are missing

The button "Footage is in" refuses: Write the editor priorities and the deadline first. The morning-after handover still runs, because an editor is named: the card goes to them without a due date, and the "Footage should be in" email has no due date. Add the deadline on the shoot afterwards and it fills the card's empty due date.

You name the editor after Go, or swap editors

Naming an editor on a shared or confirmed shoot hands them the card at once and emails You're on [shoot]. Swapping editors: the card keeps whoever already holds it. The handover only ever fills an empty owner, so reassign the card on the Editor page with "Hand to" if the wrong person has it.

The shoot was already filmed and never planned here

Do not make a plan. On the Editor page, press New card and type the shoot's name. That makes a footage-only shoot: already shot, no plan, landing straight in Footage in with "No plan — footage only". It never needs a share, Go or a reminder.

Not everyone has acknowledged the plan

Go is refused: 2 of 3 on the shoot have not acknowledged the plan. The crew's email has the acknowledge link; the editor presses "I've read the plan" on their card. The card shows "read 1 of 3". If someone left, remove them from "Who is on this shoot" and the count drops.

The plan is late, but the shoot must go ahead

Under 7 days and shared late, Go is refused for an account manager. A super admin sees Go anyway (super admin) and a box "Why is it going ahead late?". The reason is required, is stamped on the shoot ("Went ahead late — [reason]"), and the managers and Ops are emailed Went ahead late: [shoot].

The client needs to see and approve the plan

Press Share the plan with the client (needs the nine parts). It turns the portal switch on, stamps "With the client since [date]" and emails the client Your shoot plan: [shoot] — [date]. The line under it then reads Waiting on the client until they press Approve or Request changes on their portal. Their answer, with any note, appears on the shoot page and in What happened. Sharing again clears their answer and waits on them again.

Copy portal link copies the client's portal. Copy board link copies a page with only the planning board, turns both portal switches on, and lets the client comment on any board card. Their comments land on the same card on your shoot page and email the account manager.

The "Visible on the client portal" switch only shows or hides the shoot. It does not send anything and does not stamp a share.

Moving a shoot backwards

Drag the card back, or press Move…. Allowed for the people who can manage the shoot. Every stamp past the column you drop it in is cleared: back to Shared clears Go and the reminder; back to Draft clears the share too. Two refusals: nothing moves out of Shoot day ("change the shoot date to move it"), and nothing moves into Shoot day by hand.

The shoot date changes

Change it on the plan. The Shoot day and Footage in columns follow the calendar. Reminders and the morning-after handover use the new date. A confirmed shoot's date is a commitment: the app asks "Why is the date moving?" and logs the reason.

Deleting a shoot

Delete on the shoot page. The shoot is removed and the deletion logged under your name. Its cards on the Editor and Post approval boards are not deleted: they lose their "From the shoot" line and stay where they are.
